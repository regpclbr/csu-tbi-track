Privacy notice

CSU-TBI Track · CSU Technology Business Incubator, Caraga State University

What we collect

Your name, email address, contact number (optional), designation / position, office / agency, participant category and your answers on the registration form; your signature (drawn on the screen, a picture you upload, or your typed name); whether you will attend or cancelled; the days you attended and the time you checked in; and, after the activity, your evaluation answers, the name and email for your certificate, and any photos (JPG or PNG) or PDF files you choose to add to your evaluation.

Why we collect it

This is a review version of CSU-TBI Track: what you enter here is used for testing and evaluating CSU-TBI Track during the researchers' capstone study (User Acceptance Testing). Within that test, your details are used to run the activity, estimate the meals, record attendance on each day, print your signature on the activity's Attendance and Confirmation of Attendance forms (which replace the paper forms), send you your evaluation link and your Certificate of Participation, and prepare the TBI's activity reports (reports show totals, not names). Photos and files you add to your evaluation may be used in TBI reports (activity, evaluation and accomplishment reports) once TBI staff select them; please do not add photos of people who did not agree. Photos are saved without their hidden details (such as the phone's location); PDF files are kept as you uploaded them. After each check-in we email you a short notice, so you know if someone else checked in with your email.

Who can see it

Only authorized TBI staff who sign in to CSU-TBI Track (your evaluation photos and files: only staff who organize activities). Other participants never see who registered. Your details are not sold and are not shared outside Caraga State University and NAVIGATU, except when the law requires it.

How long we keep it

Data entered in this review version is kept on the researchers' computer until you ask for it to be deleted. To ask, contact the researchers listed below. Your signature image (or typed name) is deleted automatically once the activity's Attendance, Confirmation of Attendance and Activity Report are kept, and at the latest one year after the activity; the printed forms keep your signature as official records. Photos and files you add to your evaluation are kept with your evaluation (they are report material) and are deleted with the activity.

Your rights

Under the Data Privacy Act of 2012 (Republic Act No. 10173) you may ask to see, correct or delete your details, or withdraw your consent. Contact: Regine S. Paclibar and Chrizza Patricia E. Mandap, BSIT, CCIS, Caraga State University, through the project email shown in the invitation emails.

Version November 25, 2026.
