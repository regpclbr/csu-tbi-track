Privacy notice

CSU-TBI Track · CSU Technology Business Incubator, Caraga State University

What we collect

Your name, email address, contact number (optional), participant category and your answers on the registration form; whether you attended; and, after the activity, your evaluation answers and the name and email for your certificate.

Why we collect it

This is a review version of CSU-TBI Track: what you enter here is used for testing and evaluating CSU-TBI Track during the researchers' capstone study (User Acceptance Testing). Within that test, your details are used to run the activity, record attendance, send you your evaluation link and your Certificate of Participation, and prepare the TBI's activity reports (reports show totals, not names).

Who can see it

Only authorized TBI staff who sign in to CSU-TBI Track. Other participants never see who registered. Your details are not sold and are not shared outside Caraga State University and NAVIGATU, except when the law requires it.

How long we keep it

In this review version, data entered in this review version is kept only until the end of the study and deleted no later than November 30, 2026.

Your rights

Under the Data Privacy Act of 2012 (Republic Act No. 10173) you may ask to see, correct or delete your details, or withdraw your consent. Contact: Regine S. Paclibar and Chrizza Patricia E. Mandap, BSIT, CCIS, Caraga State University, through the project email shown in the invitation emails.

Version November 17, 2026.
