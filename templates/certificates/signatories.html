{% extends "layouts/app.html" %} {% comment %} /certificates/signatories/ (certificates.page_views.signatories, can_approve_certificates; ASSUMPTIONS 363): the list of people who sign certificates, built by certificates.pages.signatories_page. Every change goes through certificates.services: add_signatory, edit_signatory (in place while no certificate uses the person, otherwise a new version), replace_signature (a replaced image is a new version; a white background is made transparent, with a preview), remove_signature_background ("Remove white background" for an existing image: a new version, ASSUMPTIONS 366), deactivate_signatory (refused for an active default), set_default_signatories. The Edit and Signature dialogs show the impact of a new version (services.version_impact_text). Nobody is ever deleted (there is no delete URL). Signature thumbnails come from certificates:signature_image. {% endcomment %} {% block content %}

The people whose name, position and signature are printed on certificates. Each activity chooses two of them (left and right) on its Certificates tab. A person already printed on a certificate is never changed: a new signature image or a new name or position becomes a new version that every activity uses from then on, and issued certificates keep the old one until they are reissued.
